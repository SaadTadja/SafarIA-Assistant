# Travel Documents

## Passport Requirements

A valid passport is required for all international flights. Many countries require the passport to remain valid for at least 6 months beyond the intended date of return, and to have at least one blank visa page. Passengers should check the specific requirements of both their destination and any transit countries.

## Visa Requirements

Visa requirements depend on the passenger's nationality, destination, and purpose of travel. Some destinations offer visa-on-arrival or electronic travel authorization (eTA/ESTA-style systems) for eligible nationalities, which should be obtained before departure. It is the passenger's responsibility to verify visa requirements; the airline is not liable for denied boarding due to missing travel documents.

## Identity Documents for Domestic Travel

For domestic flights, a valid national ID card or passport is generally sufficient. Some domestic routes may also accept other government-issued photo identification.

## Minors Traveling

Minors traveling internationally, especially when not accompanied by both parents, may need additional documents such as a parental consent letter or, in some jurisdictions, a family record book. Requirements vary significantly by country and should be verified with the relevant consulate.

## Health and Vaccination Documents

Certain destinations require proof of specific vaccinations (for example, yellow fever) or health declarations. Passengers should check current requirements for their destination well before travel, as these can change with limited notice.

## Document Checks at the Airport

Airline staff will verify passport, visa, and other required documents at check-in. Passengers without the correct documents for their destination may be denied boarding, and in such cases standard refund and change fees will apply since the airline is not responsible for document verification failures.
