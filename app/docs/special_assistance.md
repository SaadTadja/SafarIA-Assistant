# Special Assistance

## Requesting Assistance

Passengers requiring special assistance, including reduced mobility (PRM), visual or hearing impairment, or other medical needs, should request assistance at the time of booking or at least 48 hours before departure to ensure availability of the required support.

## Wheelchair Assistance

Wheelchair assistance is available from the airport entrance to the aircraft seat, and from the aircraft to the airport exit or connecting gate, at no additional charge. Passengers can indicate whether they can walk short distances, climb stairs, or need to remain in a wheelchair for the entire journey, so the airline can prepare appropriate support.

## Traveling with Medical Equipment

Passengers traveling with medical equipment (oxygen concentrators, CPAP machines, mobility scooters) must notify the airline in advance. Battery-powered mobility devices may require specific battery documentation for safety reasons. Most medical equipment travels free of charge in addition to standard baggage allowance.

## Traveling with a Service Animal

Passengers traveling with a certified service animal should notify the airline at least 48 hours before departure and provide any documentation required by the destination country's import regulations.

## Assistance for Unaccompanied Minors

Children traveling alone (unaccompanied minors) can be enrolled in the airline's unaccompanied minor service, which provides supervision from check-in through to handover to the receiving guardian at the destination. This service typically applies to children between 5 and 12 years old and may be mandatory up to a certain age.

## Medical Conditions and Fitness to Fly

Passengers with certain medical conditions (recent surgery, unstable cardiac conditions, late-stage pregnancy) may be required to provide a medical certificate confirming fitness to fly before boarding. Requirements vary by condition and airline; passengers should check well in advance of travel.
