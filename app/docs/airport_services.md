# Airport Services

## Lounges

Passengers traveling in Business or First Class, and eligible frequent-flyer tier members, have access to airport lounges offering seating, food and beverages, Wi-Fi, and shower facilities where available. Lounge access can also be purchased separately at most major airports.

## Wi-Fi and Connectivity

Most airports provide free Wi-Fi in terminal areas, typically requiring registration with an email address or phone number. Premium high-speed Wi-Fi may be available for purchase in some terminals.

## Parking

Short-term and long-term parking is available at most airports, with long-term parking offering discounted daily rates for trips longer than 3 days. Pre-booking parking online is usually cheaper than paying at the gate on arrival.

## Currency Exchange and Banking

Currency exchange counters and ATMs are available in most airport terminals, typically located both before and after security. Exchange rates at the airport are often less favorable than those at banks in the city center.

## Dining and Shopping

Airport terminals typically offer a range of dining options and duty-free shopping after security. Duty-free purchases of liquids over 100ml are allowed in carry-on baggage only if sealed in a tamper-evident bag provided at the point of purchase, and may be subject to additional screening at connecting airports.

## Transfers and Connections

Passengers with connecting flights at the same airport should check whether their connection requires re-clearing security or immigration, particularly for international-to-domestic connections. Minimum connection times vary by airport and are shown at the time of booking.
