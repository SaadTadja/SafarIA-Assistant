Your layover, a moment of serenity

Transform your transit time into a privileged moment of comfort at Mohammed V Airport

Transit Lounge access: follow the floor markings and pass through police control in the transit area. You can proceed directly to the boarding gate without waiting. Please check the airport information screens to verify your boarding gate.

Services provided to connecting passengers: new meal options. For your comfort, catering options offered to transit passengers with a layover of more than 4 hours (for passengers connecting to an international flight) have been redesigned. You can dine at various outlets in Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 of Mohammed V International Airport: Café Verro, Pomme de Pain, Le Medina, Illy or Mr. Brochette. You can compose your meal up to 130 MAD for lunch and dinner, and 60 MAD for breakfast.

A short nap during your transit: the Oasis Lounge Transit area at Terminal 2 of Mohammed V Airport offers rest and relaxation areas with lounge chairs, a dedicated kids' area, and a business center for quiet meetings.

Transit between 4 and 8 hours: a complimentary meal service is offered in the restaurant of your choice. Present yourself at the restaurant with your boarding pass - a voucher is no longer required. Breakfast between 5:00 a.m. and 10:00 a.m., lunch between 11:30 a.m. and 2:30 p.m., dinner between 7:00 p.m. and 10:00 p.m.

Transit of more than 8 hours (not authorized to leave Casablanca airport): complimentary meal service as above. Business Class passengers may, subject to availability, benefit from a hotel room (accommodation outside the airport is subject to a transit visa or exit permit).

Transit of more than 12 hours (not authorized to leave Casablanca airport): complimentary meal service as above. Economy Class passengers may, subject to availability, benefit from a hotel room (same visa/exit permit condition).

Transit of more than 8 hours (authorized to leave Casablanca airport): complimentary meal service as above. Business Class passengers may, subject to availability communicated by the Transit & Accommodation agency at Terminal 2, benefit from a hotel room outside the airport.

Transit of more than 12 hours (authorized to leave Casablanca airport): complimentary meal service as above. Economy Class passengers may, subject to availability communicated by the Transit & Accommodation agency at Terminal 2, benefit from a hotel room outside the airport.

Royal Air Maroc lounges - Medina Lounge: the domestic lounge at Casablanca, designed with warm colors, traditional patterns, and local materials for a relaxing experience during domestic travel, with local cuisine available.

Royal Air Maroc Lounge at Terminal 1: a redesigned space with a refreshed layout, culinary experience, bar, and terrace.

Orly: Zenith VIP Lounge - inspired by Moroccan culture, includes a catering area, a large business corner, a terrace with a panoramic view of the tarmac, and a prayer area.

Marrakech: L'Oasis VIP Lounge - approximately 500 m², with a buffet, rest areas, a relaxation area, and workspaces with computers for business travelers.

Lounge conditions of admission: accessible to passengers holding a business class boarding card, and passengers with Safar Flyer Platinum, Ambassador, or Gold loyalty cards.

Online check-in allows you to choose your seat in advance, download or print your boarding pass, and modify or cancel your online check-in before the deadline. This service is available for most eligible destinations and airports. When departing from Morocco, you must still proceed to the check-in counter to collect your boarding pass, as required by Moroccan authorities.
