Flight information for passengers with reduced mobility: Royal Air Maroc staff provide information on the location of seats with movable armrests, the location of seats that cannot be assigned to persons with reduced mobility (including exit rows), the availability of equipment and level boarding, limitations on loading mobility equipment or assistive devices in the cabin or hold (including wheelchair loading), whether the aircraft is equipped with accessible lavatories, and the availability of services for persons with reduced mobility on board.

Safety Assistant: a safety assistant will be required by Royal Air Maroc to accompany a passenger during the flight if the passenger is traveling on a stretcher, is unable to understand or respond properly to safety instructions, or has a level of physical mobility so restricted that they could not evacuate in an emergency. A safety assistant must be at least 12 years old, willing and physically capable of participating in the passenger's evacuation, and able to understand and apply safety instructions on the passenger's behalf. The safety assistant may be a travel companion who meets these conditions, seated next to the passenger. A safety assistant cannot simultaneously accompany a person with reduced mobility and an infant or child under 5, and cannot assist multiple reduced-mobility passengers at once.

Portable Oxygen Concentrators (POC): passengers may bring or use their own POC on board if it is FAA-approved. The passenger must carry enough batteries to power the POC for up to 150% of the maximum expected flight duration, packed according to applicable safety regulations (zip-lock bags). Royal Air Maroc will inform passengers of the maximum expected flight duration within 48 and/or 24 hours following booking, prior to departure.

Approved POC models include: AirSep FreeStyle, AirSep LifeStyle, AirSep Focus, AirSep FreeStyle 5, Delphi RS-00400, DeVilbiss Healthcare iGo, Inogen One, Inogen One G2, Inogen One G3, Inova Labs LifeChoice, Inova Labs LifeChoice Activox, International Biophysics LifeChoice, Invacare XPO2, Invacare Solo2, Oxlife Independence Oxygen Concentrator, Oxus RS-00400, Precision Medical EasyPulse, Respironics EverGo, Respironics SimplyGo, SeQual Eclipse, SeQual eQuinox, SeQual Oxywell, SeQual SAROS, and VBOX Trooper.

POC requirements: the device must meet applicable requirements for portable electronic medical devices; it must be able to be stowed and used in the cabin per TSA/FAA/PHMSA regulations; the passenger must carry enough batteries for 150% of flight duration; the passenger must declare the POC and have it noted in the booking record 48 hours before departure; and the passenger must present a medical statement from their physician confirming they can use the POC appropriately (a photocopy is acceptable).

Medical certificate required when: traveling on a stretcher or in an incubator, requiring therapeutic oxygen during the flight, having a contagious disease, or when a medical condition gives rise to reasonable doubt about traveling safely without medical assistance.

Advance notification required: 72 hours before the flight for medical oxygen provided by the airline; 48 hours before the flight for use of a personal Portable Oxygen Concentrator. Also requiring prior notification: transport of an incubator, stretcher (STCR), a group of 10 or more passengers with reduced mobility traveling together, provision of an on-board wheelchair (WCOB), transport of a service dog on a flight of 8 hours or more, transport of a passenger who is both visually and hearing impaired, and transport of any dangerous goods requiring special packaging.

Special meals (gluten-free, low-cholesterol, etc.) can be requested at the time of booking.

General assistance conditions: assistance requests can be made online. If a passenger's state of health requires medical clearance from Royal Air Maroc, ticket issuance depends on that clearance, and payment can only proceed once travel authorization is given. Safar Flyer loyalty miles are not valid for payment of assistance services (stretcher, extra seat, etc.), except for the UM (unaccompanied minor) service, which can be paid with Safar Flyer miles. For code-share flights operated by partner airlines, contact the Call Center or nearest RAM agency to confirm assistance availability. Depending on health condition, medical clearance from RAM's medical service may be required, and the presence of an accompanying person may also be required. Assistance needs should be signaled at least 48 hours before departure; after this deadline, availability of adapted services cannot be guaranteed.
