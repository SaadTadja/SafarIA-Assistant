Source: Royal Air Maroc "Expectant Mother's Disclaimer of Responsibility" form.

Expectant mothers must complete a disclaimer of responsibility form declaring their gestational age and confirming they have no pregnancy-related complications before travel.

Expectant mothers beyond eight (8) months of gestation, and those who suffer from any complication or difficulty due to pregnancy regardless of the age of gestation, are required to submit a personal physician's clearance certificate before they are allowed to travel by air.

For expectant mothers who are minors (below the legal age of majority), the disclaimer form must be co-signed by their husband, parent, or guardian.
