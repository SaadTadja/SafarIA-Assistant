Excess Baggage Rates
Excess baggage charges (per kg, per piece, or special item) are applied at check-in. The charge is calculated based on the difference between your authorized baggage allowance and the actual weight or dimensions of your baggage.

Important Note: This fee applies to all flights, except for routes: between Turkey and Africa, to/from North America, to/from Laayoune/Dakhla (via Las Palmas), and to/from Saudi Arabia.
