Your Baggage Guide: Before and After

All the essential information for a stress-free management of your personal belongings

Before Your Departure

Identification
Label your bags with your contact details and place an ID tag inside.


Clearance
Remove all old luggage tags from previous flights.


Security
Use TSA-approved locks for your trips to/from the United States.


Precaution
Keep your medications, documents, and valuables with you in the cabin.


Compliance
Make sure you do not carry any prohibited items.


Upon Your Arrival

Verification
Check your bags before leaving the airport to avoid mistakenly taking another passenger's luggage.


Reporting
In case of undelivered baggage, contact the "Baggage Service" desk to fill out a PIR report before leaving the airport.
