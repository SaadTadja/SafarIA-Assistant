Your baggage guide: Everything about checked baggage

Properly preparing your bags is the first step of your journey. Checked baggage belongs to the passengers and is under the responsibility of the carrier who issues the registration tag.

Your baggage allowances: The golden rules
You benefit from a predetermined allowance that may vary depending on the fare, routing, passenger category, and class. It is based on three standard parameters:

Weight
The maximum weight must not exceed 32 kg.
Volume
The sum of the three dimensions (H+L+W) must not exceed 203 cm.
Number
0 to 3 pieces depending on the flight, class, and Safar Flyer status.


Acceptance conditions
At check-in, the agent in charge may refuse to transport baggage that:

Is unsuitable: Baggage that is insufficiently packed or unsuitable for air travel.
Is non-compliant: Ball-shaped baggage without a flat surface, or packed in a non-compliant manner.
Presents a danger: Risk to the aircraft hold due to its packaging, content, or nature.
Exceeds limits: Baggage that exceeds the authorized checked baggage allowances.
0 to 3 pieces
Depending on the flight number, passenger class, and loyalty status (Safar Flyer).

kg
23 or 32 kg
The standard weight must not exceed 23 or 32 kg, depending on the passenger class and loyalty status (Safar Brochure).

203 cm
The sum of the three dimensions must not exceed 203 cm (height + width + length).

New Piece Concept baggage policy
Baggage allowance on Royal Air Maroc flights is defined according to the Piece Concept rule. Weight cannot be shared or averaged between different bags: the authorized weight limit applies individually to each piece. Any baggage item weighing over 32 kg or exceeding 203 cm must be checked through the Cargo service.

Packing requirements
✓ Effectively protect the transported items
✓ Restricting to the weight and nature of the item
✓ Easy to handle and free of any rough edges or sharp parts
✓ Durable enough to withstand standard handling
Safety and security advice
Baggage check-in is individual and nominal. We highly recommend attaching a RAM identification tag to your baggage. Please keep the following items with you:

•
Money, jewelry, precious metals
•
Securities, bonds, business documents
•
Valuable items
•
Fragile items (crystal, glass)
•
Perishable goods
•
Medications
